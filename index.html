Your data is stored in this browser
Positions, cash, history, theses, conviction scores, rules, your API key and your Worker URL are kept in this browser's localStorage. There is no account and no Hardcap server. Two things leave the browser, and only when you use them: ticker symbols go to your Cloudflare Worker for prices, fundamentals and earnings calls, and when you run the IC or Ask Claude, your holdings, cash, rules and theses are sent to Anthropic's API under your own key. Clear your browser data and it is all gone, so export a JSON backup after each import.
